Privacy Policy

SIMA Power BI Reporting
Last updated: 29-OCTOBER-2026

SIMA Power BI Reporting (the "App") is an internal tool operated by SOCIAL INVESTMENT MANAGERS AND ADVISORS ("SIMA", "we"). It is not offered to the public or to third parties.

What the App accesses

With the authorization of a SIMA QuickBooks administrator, the App reads accounting reports (such as the General Ledger) and related account balances from SIMA's own QuickBooks Online company. The App does not access any other company's data. It only reads data and does not create, change or delete records in QuickBooks.

How we use the data

The data is used to refresh SIMA's internal financial dashboards in Microsoft Power BI, for management reporting, controlling and monitoring.

Storage and security

Data is transferred over HTTPS and stored only in SIMA-controlled locations that are accessible to authorized finance personnel. QuickBooks access credentials (OAuth tokens) are kept in a secure secrets store, are never published in source code, and are not shared with anyone.

Sharing

We do not sell the data or use it for advertising. Data is shared only with SIMA's authorized personnel and with service providers that host or process it on SIMA's behalf, under confidentiality obligations.

Retention

Data is kept for as long as needed for SIMA's financial reporting and record-keeping requirements.

Disconnecting

A QuickBooks administrator can revoke the App's access at any time from the connected-apps settings of the QuickBooks Online account. Once access is revoked, the App stops receiving data and the stored access credentials are deleted.
