SIMA Power BI Reporting

SIMA Power BI Reporting is an internal tool operated by SOCIAL INVESTMENT MANAGERS AND ADVISORS LLC ("SIMA"). With the authorization of a SIMA QuickBooks administrator, it reads accounting reports, such as the General Ledger, from SIMA's own QuickBooks Online company and uses them to refresh SIMA's internal financial dashboards in Microsoft Power BI.

This tool is for internal use by authorized SIMA personnel only. It is not offered to the public or to third parties.
